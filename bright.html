Building a New Nation

“Freedom was only the beginning.”

  • IndependenceOn 15 August 1947, India began governing itself after nearly two centuries of colonial rule.
  • Integration of princely statesHundreds of princely states were brought into the union, forming a single political map.
  • Building national institutionsCourts, civil services and a central bank were established to run the new republic.
  • Establishing democratic governanceA parliamentary system was put in place to govern one of the world's most diverse populations.
  • Beginning the Republic's journeyThe groundwork laid in these early years would shape the next seven decades.

  • India becomes a RepublicThe Constitution came into force, formally making India a sovereign republic.
  • Fundamental RightsThe Constitution guaranteed basic rights and freedoms to every citizen.
  • Universal adult franchiseEvery adult citizen, regardless of background, was granted the right to vote.
  • Parliamentary democracyA system of elected representation and accountable government was established.
