Dark Side of India

The chapters history cannot ignore.

Independence gave India a new beginning. But the decades that followed also carried conflict, tragedy, inequality and difficult questions.

1947

Freedom Came With a Price

  • Partition aftermathThe drawing of a new border split Punjab and Bengal almost overnight, uprooting communities who had lived side by side for generations.
  • Refugee crisisAn estimated ten to twenty million people crossed the new border in both directions within months, one of the largest mass migrations in history.
  • Communal violenceThe migration was accompanied by widespread violence between communities, with casualty estimates that historians still debate.
  • Families separatedMany families were split across the new border, with some members never able to reunite or reconnect.
  • Mass displacementEntire neighbourhoods and villages emptied as residents fled, leaving homes, land and belongings behind.
  • Rebuilding lives in a new nationRefugees resettled in unfamiliar cities, often starting again from nothing in the new republic's earliest, most fragile years.

Wars & the Borders

A Nation Defending Its Borders

1947–48First India–Pakistan War

Fought over Kashmir within months of independence, it ended with a UN-brokered ceasefire and a disputed line of control.

1962Sino-Indian War

A brief but costly border war with China in the Himalayas exposed gaps in India's military preparedness.

1965India–Pakistan War

A second major conflict over Kashmir ended in a stalemate and the Tashkent Agreement.

1971India–Pakistan War

The war that led to the creation of Bangladesh, following a refugee crisis from East Pakistan.

1999Kargil War

A high-altitude conflict in Kashmir after infiltration across the Line of Control, fought under intense international scrutiny.

1975

1975 – 1977

The Emergency

Declared

A state of Emergency was declared, suspending ordinary constitutional protections across the country.

Civil Liberties

Civil liberties were curtailed and press censorship was imposed on newspapers and broadcasters.

Arrests

Political opponents were arrested, and a controversial mass sterilisation campaign drew lasting criticism.

1977

Elections returned in 1977, ending the Emergency and testing the resilience of India's democracy.

1984

A Year of Trauma

June

Operation Blue Star

A military operation to remove armed militants from the Golden Temple complex in Amritsar left deep and lasting wounds within the Sikh community.

31 October

Assassination of Indira Gandhi

Prime Minister Indira Gandhi was assassinated by two of her own bodyguards, an act widely linked to the events earlier that year.

Following Days

Anti-Sikh Violence

In the days that followed, organised mob violence targeted Sikh communities in Delhi and other cities, killing thousands.

  • Human costThousands of lives were lost across the events of 1984, with many more families permanently affected.
  • Communities affectedSikh communities in particular bore the brunt of the violence that followed the assassination.
  • Long struggle for justiceLegal proceedings over the 1984 violence have continued for decades, with many cases still unresolved.

2–3 December 1984

The Bhopal Gas Disaster

  • Toxic gas leakA leak of methyl isocyanate gas from a pesticide plant spread across the sleeping city of Bhopal in the middle of the night.
  • Thousands of deathsThousands died within days, with estimates of the eventual death toll varying by source and still debated today.
  • SurvivorsHundreds of thousands of residents were exposed, many suffering immediate and chronic injuries.
  • Long-term health effectsSurvivors and their children have reported respiratory, and other health problems for decades afterward.
  • Environmental contaminationSoil and groundwater around the abandoned plant remained contaminated for years after the leak.
  • Accountability debateQuestions over corporate responsibility, compensation and cleanup have continued in Indian and international courts.

Terror on Indian Soil

Attacks That Shook the Nation

1993

Bombay Bombings

A coordinated series of bomb blasts struck the city on a single day, among the deadliest attacks in India's history.

2001

Indian Parliament Attack

An armed assault on the Parliament complex in New Delhi brought India and Pakistan close to open conflict.

2006

Mumbai Train Bombings

A series of bomb blasts on the city's commuter rail network during evening rush hour killed and injured hundreds of passengers.

2008

26/11 Mumbai Attacks

A multi-day siege across several locations in Mumbai, including two hotels and a railway station, drew international attention.

2016

Uri Attack

An attack on an army base in Jammu and Kashmir killed a number of soldiers and heightened tensions along the border.

2019

Pulwama Attack

A suicide bombing targeting a security convoy in Jammu and Kashmir killed dozens of personnel and led to a sharp escalation in regional tensions.

The Price of Development

Environmental Cost

Clean India
Polluted India
  • Air pollutionMany Indian cities regularly rank among the most polluted in the world during winter months.
  • Polluted riversRivers including the Ganga and Yamuna carry heavy industrial and untreated waste in many stretches.
  • Water scarcityGroundwater depletion threatens long-term water security for both cities and farms.
  • DeforestationForest cover has been lost to agriculture, mining and infrastructure expansion.
  • Waste & plasticRapidly growing cities generate waste faster than collection and recycling systems can manage.
  • Rapid urbanisationFast-growing cities often outpace the planning needed to support them sustainably.
  • Climate vulnerabilityHeatwaves, erratic monsoons and rising seas increasingly threaten vulnerable communities.

Crimes That Forced India to Question Itself

Safety, Justice & Society

  • Crimes against womenViolence against women has remained a persistent, widely reported concern across the country.
  • The 2012 Delhi caseA brutal assault on a young woman in Delhi became a national flashpoint, drawing sustained public attention to how such crimes are handled.
  • Nationwide protestsThe case triggered large, sustained protests across Indian cities demanding justice and reform.
  • Demand for stronger lawsPublic pressure led to amended laws on sexual violence, including faster trial processes.
  • Public safety debateThe case reopened debate over women's safety in public spaces and the role of policing.
  • Continuing questions about justiceYears later, questions remain about how consistently reforms have been implemented nationwide.

The Digital Age

A New Kind of Crisis

  • MisinformationFalse or misleading content spreads quickly across messaging apps and social platforms.
  • Online hateHarassment and hate speech targeting individuals and communities have grown alongside internet access.
  • CybercrimeHacking, identity theft and online scams have risen with the country's rapid digital expansion.
  • Digital fraudFinancial scams exploiting digital payments affect millions of new internet users each year.
  • Privacy concernsData collection by apps, platforms and services has raised ongoing questions about consent and protection.
  • Social-media polarisationAlgorithm-driven feeds have been linked to deepening social and political divides.
