History of my India

Carved from stone by hands long gone, India's temples and pillars hold 5,000 years of memory in their patterns.

From the Maurya and Gupta empires to the intricate hall traditions of Jain and Hindu shrines, every arch tells of artisans who shaped devotion into architecture.

That same spirit of resilience carried India through its freedom struggle, led by voices like Mahatma Gandhi, to independence in 194 `7.
